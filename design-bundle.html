Help

  1. Tap INSERT BOTTLE to open the hatch.
  2. Drop in one clean, empty PET bottle. Caps and labels are fine.
  3. Bigger bottles earn more time: 1L 10 min, Mismo 7 min, Sakto 5 min.
  4. Wait for the machine to confirm. Your Wi-Fi time is added straight away.
  5. Repeat for more time, then tap Finish deposit.

Connect to the BottleNet Wi-Fi network on your device to use your time.
